Frequently Asked Questions

Is my Telegram account safe? ▼

Yes, 100% safe. TG Drive communicates directly with Telegram servers using the official MTProto protocol, just like the official Telegram mobile and desktop clients. Your password and private messages are never accessed or stored on third-party servers.

Is cloud storage truly unlimited? ▼

Yes! Telegram imposes no overall limit on total data uploaded to Saved Messages and private channels. You can safely store hundreds of gigabytes or terabytes of personal media and documents.

Can Telegram or anyone else view my files? ▼

No! When client-side encryption is enabled in settings, your device locks files with AES-256-GCM before uploading. Telegram's servers only receive scrambled, unreadable chunks that can only be decrypted by your local key.

What is the app download size? ▼

TG Drive is engineered to be extremely lightweight — only ~11.8 MB in size. It downloads in seconds and runs smoothly even on budget Android devices.
